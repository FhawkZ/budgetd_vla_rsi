The archive contains our experiment launch, transport and audit scripts recovered from Dex_operation commit b329763c24cf6f4628442a0ad42c2a43e6bb2711; archive/manifest.json records byte hashes. No new license is assigned to upstream code. Independent project licensing remains to be chosen by the owner before public release.
